# Medical Information Response

*Attack-Free Outcomes and Duration of Follow-Up with Lonvoguran Ziclumeran (Lonvo-Z / NTLA-2002) in Hereditary Angioedema*

**Document ID:** SRL-2026-0001  ·  **Version:** 0.4 DRAFT  ·  **Date:** 17 July 2026  ·  **Category:** efficacy

## Summary

> **Lonvoguran ziclumeran (lonvo-z) is an investigational agent that has not been
> approved by the FDA, EMA, or any other health authority; its safety and efficacy
> have not been established. "Cure" is not an endpoint that has been evaluated in
> the lonvo-z clinical program.** This response is provided in answer to an
> unsolicited request for medical information and is presented in a balanced,
> non-promotional manner.

- **Attack-free (Phase 3 HAELO):** 32/52 (62%) with lonvo-z vs 3/28 (11%) with placebo, Week 5–28.¹
- **Attack-rate reduction (HAELO):** −87% vs placebo (P<0.001).¹
- **Long-term durability (open-label analysis):** 26/28 (93%) attack-free and prophylaxis-free at latest follow-up; mean 95% attack-rate reduction.²

- **Safety (HAELO):** all adverse events Grade 1–2; no serious or Grade ≥3 adverse events in the lonvo-z group.¹

## Attack-Free Outcomes ("Cure Rate")

Lonvoguran ziclumeran is an investigational therapy, and **"cure" is not an
endpoint that has been evaluated or established in the lonvo-z clinical program**.
The most relevant available measures are the proportion of patients who became
**attack-free** and the **reduction in attack rate**, reported below from the
pivotal Phase 3 trial together with longer-term follow-up.

**Phase 3 HAELO trial (pivotal, placebo-controlled).** In this double-blind trial,
patients received a single intravenous dose of lonvo-z 50 mg (n=52) or placebo
(n=28). From Week 5 through Week 28, 32 of 52 patients (62%; 95% CI, 47 to 75) in
the lonvo-z group were attack-free, compared with 3 of 28 patients (11%; 95% CI, 2
to 28) in the placebo group (odds ratio, 12.81; 95% CI, 3.45 to 47.55; P<0.001).
The least-squares mean monthly attack rate over the same period was 0.26 (95% CI,
0.15 to 0.45) with lonvo-z versus 2.10 (95% CI, 1.55 to 2.86) with placebo — a
relative reduction of 87% (95% CI, −93 to −78; P<0.001).¹

## Long-Term Durability

Across all 28 patients who received lonvo-z 50 mg, 26 of 28 (93%) were attack-free
and free of long-term prophylaxis at the latest follow-up, for a median of 10.2
months (range, 0.5 to 24.0), with a mean 95% reduction in monthly attack rate from
baseline. These are single-arm, open-label data reported in a congress abstract
(not a full peer-reviewed manuscript) and are not placebo-controlled.²

## Duration of Available Follow-Up Data

The longest available follow-up comes from the open-label analysis of the 28
patients treated with lonvo-z 50 mg: as of the 12 February 2025 data cutoff, the
median follow-up after the 50-mg dose was 18.9 months (range, 10.2 to 24.4) in the
cohort dosed initially at 50 mg — representing **up to approximately 2 years**.²
In the pivotal, placebo-controlled Phase 3 HAELO trial, the median follow-up was
7.5 months (range, 4.9 to 12.8) as of the 10 February 2026 data cutoff.¹
Longer-term and ongoing follow-up continue to accrue.

> **Data gap identified:** The available lonvo-z source documents report follow-up
> of up to approximately two years; durability beyond this period is not yet
> established. Requests for longer-term durability data are flagged for the Medical
> Information team for follow-up as additional data become available.

## Safety (Phase 3 HAELO)

Adverse events during or after infusion were reported in 92% of lonvo-z and 86% of
placebo recipients through Week 28; the most common event was infusion-related
reaction (62% lonvo-z vs. 18% placebo). All adverse events were Grade 1 or 2, and
no serious or Grade ≥3 adverse events were reported in the lonvo-z group.¹
