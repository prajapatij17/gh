## What each source covers (quick data map)

Use this to route MI questions to the right source. All studies are of the same
agent — NTLA-2002 (the development code), now named **lonvoguran ziclumeran
(lonvo-z)** — funded by Intellia Therapeutics.

### NEJM-2024-PH1 — Phase 1 dose-escalation (Longhurst et al.)
- **Design:** phase 1 dose-escalation portion of a combined phase 1–2 trial
  (NCT05120830); single IV dose 25 mg (n=3), 50 mg (n=4), 75 mg (n=3). Primary
  endpoints: safety / side-effect profile. Secondary/exploratory: PK, PD, clinical
  efficacy (investigator-confirmed attacks).
- **Safety:** most common AEs infusion-related reactions and fatigue; no
  dose-limiting toxic effects, no serious AEs, no grade ≥3 AEs, no clinically
  important lab findings.
- **PD (kallikrein):** mean change in total plasma kallikrein −67% (25 mg),
  −84% (50 mg), −95% (75 mg) from baseline to latest assessment.
- **Efficacy (exploratory):** mean change in monthly attack rate wk 1–16 was
  −91% (25 mg), −97% (50 mg), −80% (75 mg); across all patients −95% through
  latest assessment.

### NEJM-2024-PH2 — Phase 2 randomized (Cohn et al.) + NEJM-2024-PH2-SUPPL
- **Design:** phase 2 portion of the phase 1–2 trial (NCT05120830); randomized
  2:2:1 to single dose lonvo-z 25 mg (n=10), 50 mg (n=11), or placebo (n=6).
  Primary endpoint: monthly attack rate wk 1–16. Secondary: safety, PK, PD.
- **Efficacy:** estimated mean monthly attack rate 0.70 (95% CI 0.25–1.98) at
  25 mg, 0.65 (95% CI 0.24–1.76) at 50 mg, 2.82 (95% CI 0.80–9.89) placebo —
  −75% (25 mg) and −77% (50 mg) vs placebo. Attack-free wk 1–16: 40% (4/10) at
  25 mg, 73% (8/11) at 50 mg.
- **Safety:** most common AEs headache, fatigue, nasopharyngitis.
- **PD (kallikrein):** mean change in total plasma kallikrein −55% (25 mg),
  −86% (50 mg) to week 16; unchanged with placebo.
- **Supplement:** CONSORT diagram, PK of NTLA-2002 analytes, AE-QoL, responder
  status, and a phase 1 update.

### NEJM-2026-HAELO — Pivotal Phase 3 HAELO trial (Cohn et al.)
- **Design:** phase 3, double-blind (NCT06634420); randomized 2:1, patients ≥16 y
  with HAE with C1-inhibitor deficiency, single IV lonvo-z 50 mg (n=52) vs placebo
  (n=28), N=80. Primary endpoint: investigator-confirmed monthly attack rate
  wk 5–28. Median follow-up 7.5 months (range 4.9–12.8) as of 10 Feb 2026.
- **Efficacy:** LS-mean monthly attack rate 0.26 (95% CI 0.15–0.45) lonvo-z vs
  2.10 (95% CI 1.55–2.86) placebo; relative difference **−87%** (95% CI −93 to
  −78; **P<0.001**).
- **Safety:** infusion-period AEs in 92% (lonvo-z) vs 86% (placebo) through wk 28.
  Most common AEs more frequent with lonvo-z (>10%): infusion-related reaction,
  headache, fatigue, back pain, upper respiratory tract infection. **No serious or
  grade ≥3 AEs** in the lonvo-z group.
