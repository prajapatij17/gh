RATIONALE: Lonvoguran ziclumeran (lonvo-z) is an investigational, 
CRISPR-based, one-time treatment designed to rebalance the kallikrein￾kinin system in patients with hereditary angioedema (HAE) by perma￾nently editing the KLKB1 gene. Lonvo-z was evaluated in a dose-finding 
Phase 1/2 study (NCT05120830). After lonvo-z 50mg was identified as 
the Phase 3 dose, patients who received placebo or lonvo-z 25mg were 
eligible to receive lonvo-z 50mg. Herein, we describe longer-term dura￾bility/safety among all patients who received lonvo-z 50mg.
METHODS: Patients (N528) received a single infusion of lonvo-z 50mg 
(initial dose 50mg, n515; lonvo-z 25mg to 50mg [25-50mg], n57; placebo 
to lonvo-z 50mg [PBO-50mg], n56). Safety and efficacy outcomes were 
assessed.
RESULTS: As of 12Feb2025, median (range) follow-up after receiving 
lonvo-z 50mg was 18.9 (10.2-24.4), 5.6 (3.8-7.4), and 2.8 (0.5-3.6) months 
for 50mg, PBO-50mg, and 25-50mg, respectively. Following lonvo-z 
50mg infusion, the mean (SD) monthly attack rate reduction from baseline 
to the latest assessment was 95% (13.2) without long-term prophylaxis 
(LTP). At latest follow-up, 93% (26/28) of patients were attack-free and 
LTP-free for a median (range) of 10.2 (0.5-24.0) months. The most 
common adverse events (>_20%) were infusion-related reaction (50%), 
fatigue (32%), headache (21%), and upper respiratory tract infection 
(21%), all Grade 1/2 and generally transient. The safety profile for PBO￾50mg and 25-50mg after lonvo-z 50mg was consistent with initial 50mg 
dose cohort. Updated data will be presented.
CONCLUSIONS: With up to 2 years of follow-up, a one-time treatment 
with lonvo-z 50mg led to robust, sustained reductions in HAE attacks 
without the need for LTP with an acceptable safety profile.
